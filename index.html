About the studio

Creative design that helps businesses speak clearly.

Fetman Digital (PTY) LTD is a Durban-based graphic design studio helping businesses turn ideas into confident visual identities. We create branding, promotional design, corporate materials and digital visuals that communicate clearly and stand out in a crowded market.

From logo concepts and full brand systems to flyers, posters, company profiles and digital marketing assets, our work is shaped to help each client present themselves professionally and with purpose.

We work with business owners, SMEs and growing brands that want thoughtful, effective design without unnecessary complexity. Whether the need is a single flyer or a broader visual identity, the goal is always the same: make the brand easier to understand and harder to ignore.

FAQ

Common questions before we begin.

Fetman Digital provides logo design, branding and identity work, marketing and promotional design, print-ready layouts, corporate design and digital design support.

Send an enquiry through the contact form or email the studio with your business name, service required and a brief overview of your project.

Useful information includes your business name, target audience, design goals, references, required deliverables and any deadlines or brand materials you already have.

Revision rounds are agreed at project commencement and refined through the design process to ensure the final artwork matches the approved brief.

Final file formats are discussed with each client and may include editable source files and production-ready versions depending on the project.
