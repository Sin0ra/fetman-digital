About the studio

Creative design that helps businesses speak clearly.

Fetman Digital (PTY) LTD is an East London graphic design studio helping businesses turn ideas into confident visual identities. We create branding, promotional design, corporate materials and digital visuals that communicate clearly and stand out in a crowded market.

From logo concepts and full brand systems to flyers, posters, company profiles and digital marketing assets, our work is shaped to help each client present themselves professionally and with purpose.

We work with business owners, SMEs and growing brands that want thoughtful, effective design without unnecessary complexity. Whether the need is a single flyer or a broader visual identity, the goal is always the same: make the brand easier to understand and harder to ignore.

The monthly website partnership

Keep your website cared for after launch.

The setup fee covers the initial website development. The monthly subscription pays for the ongoing care listed in your approved quotation, such as maintenance, security checks, backups, routine software and dependency updates where applicable, performance monitoring, technical support and minor content updates.

Included maintenance is not charged again separately to monthly partnership customers. It does not include unlimited revisions, new features, redesigns or development. Any work beyond the included care is scoped and quoted before it starts.

How the monthly option works

  • Setup and recurring subscription are separate charges.
  • Only work described in the approved quotation or statement of work is included. Revision rounds, client approvals and supplied content responsibilities are agreed in writing.
  • Hosting management is included only when your proposal says so; domain, email and other third-party fees may be billed separately.
  • Monthly website partnerships and maintenance plans require 30 days’ written cancellation notice. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the applicable notice period. Charges remain due for services delivered and undisputed balances outstanding up to that effective date; no cancellation penalty is added.
  • After cancellation, handover arrangements are agreed in writing. Outstanding undisputed invoices must be settled before transfer of paid-for deliverables, subject to applicable law and the signed agreement.
  • Ownership and any usage or source-file rights are recorded in the project agreement.

Working terms

A clear brief protects both sides.

These website notes help you understand how projects are planned and supported. They are not a substitute for a signed quotation, statement of work or service agreement.

Scope, approvals and client responsibilities

Only work in an approved quotation or statement of work is included. Additional features, new functionality, redesigns and work beyond included maintenance hours require written approval and a separate quotation. Revision limits are agreed for each project, and client approval is required before agreed work is published or released.

Clients provide lawful content, accurate information, necessary approvals and access credentials. Clients remain responsible for rights to supplied images, logos, fonts, text and other materials. Missing content, access or approvals may affect delivery dates.

Ownership, licences and reusable tools

Client-owned materials remain the client’s materials. Paid-for deliverables, source files and any copyright assignment or licence are handled according to the signed project agreement and payment terms. Fetman Digital’s pre-existing code, tools, frameworks, methods and reusable components remain subject to that agreement. Third-party software, fonts, plugins, imagery and other assets remain subject to their licences.

Care, security and reasonable limits

Fetman Digital will use reasonable care and skill in delivering the agreed services. No website or system can be promised completely secure, continuously available or unaffected by events outside our reasonable control. We do not guarantee third-party uptime, search rankings, sales, revenue or other business outcomes.

Backup and restoration arrangements depend on the selected service and agreed scope. Any responsibility or liability is governed by the signed agreement and applicable South African law, including consumer rights and restrictions concerning gross negligence. Nothing here excludes liability that cannot legally be excluded.

Personal information and complaints

Personal information is handled in accordance with applicable data-protection law, including POPIA. Where Fetman Digital processes information on a client’s behalf, the parties’ roles, instructions, security measures and incident responsibilities should be recorded in the project agreement. Clients remain responsible for having a lawful basis and required notices for information they collect.

Raise a complaint in writing through the enquiry form with the relevant project details. The parties should exchange the facts and attempt good-faith negotiation before formal proceedings, without removing any statutory right or remedy.
